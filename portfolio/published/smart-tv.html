Smart TV

Smart TV - Re-Engaging the Multitasker

Interaction Systems · Visual-First UX · Cross-Device Experiences

Smart TV presentation slideshow

1 / 18


Role & Scope

Senior Interaction Designer for a system-level re-engagement concept for Smart TV, exploring how on-screen interactions can bring distracted viewers back into the TV experience without competing for attention.

Industry Context

Multitasking is now the default TV behavior. Over 70% of viewers use a second device while watching TV — creating moments of distraction, but also new opportunities for re-engagement.

Project Overview

This project explores how Smart TV can design a scalable re-engagement system that:

  • Lives natively within the TV UI
  • Feels supportive, not interruptive
  • Rewards attention with context, interaction, or optional participation

What I Explored -> Design Focus Areas

  • How people really watch TV today ?
  • What does the research reveal?
  • What shapes the design space & guide the experience ?
  • Where does re-engagement feel most natural?
  • System Design Experience
  • What happens after content pauses, ends, or is missed
