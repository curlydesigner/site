What I Teach

Sessions are designed to be approachable, imaginative, and useful in practice. I create structured spaces where people can explore, experiment safely, and connect ideas across disciplines, abilities, and contexts. My teaching practice spans community workshops, adult learning, creative programs, interdisciplinary collaborations, and online sessions.
