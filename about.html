My Journey

From visual storytelling to end-to-end product design.

My journey into design began with a fascination for how culture and design shape human experience, evolving naturally into a deeper empathy for human-centered design and accessibility.
This curiosity became a lifelong pursuit. I graduated from Fine Arts and Design school, later expanded this foundation through academic research and hands-on practice, earning a Bachelor’s in Design and a Master’s in Human Interaction Design.

Practice

Rooted in a deep empathy for human-centered design & accessibility, over the years, I’ve evolved from visual designer to holistic UX/UI Product Designer, shaping end-to-end experiences across the entire product life cycle. From early discovery and strategy, to prototyping, testing, and final delivery. I transform complexity into clarity through data-driven insight, user research, & strategic design thinking - Crafting engaging, functional, and accessible solutions that meet user needs & business goals across platforms. Today, I continue to grow by exploring the evolving landscape of Product Design and Human-Computer Interaction — from AI-driven adaptive interfaces to the future of mixed physical and digital experiences.

Philosophy

I’m fascinated by the intersection of creativity and technology, and how design can shape tomorrow’s human-machine relationships. My philosophy centers on creating better products for humanity. I believe design is both a responsibility and a craft — a way to make technology more accessible, empathetic, and intuitive.

Driven by empathy. Defined by imagination.
Designer is not just my title — it’s who I am!
